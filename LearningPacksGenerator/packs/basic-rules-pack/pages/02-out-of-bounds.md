# Out of Bounds

In golf, “out of bounds” refers to areas outside the course where play is not allowed. Understanding this rule will help you navigate the course more effectively.

## What Does Out of Bounds Mean?

If your ball lands outside the designated playing area—marked usually by white stakes or lines—it is considered out of bounds. In this case, you must return to the spot where you last played the shot and take a penalty stroke before playing again.

### Key Points to Remember:
- **Know the Boundaries**: Familiarise yourself with the course layout and identify areas that are out of bounds.
- **Penalty Strokes**: If your ball goes out of bounds, you incur a one-stroke penalty.
- **Re-teeing**: After assessing the situation, you will hit your next shot from the teeing area, adding an extra stroke to your score.

### Fun Fact!
Most golf courses will have markers indicating out-of-bounds areas. This can include fences, roads, or even natural features such as trees.

### Quick Tips:
- Always keep an eye on where your ball lands.
- Try to aim for the middle of the fairway to avoid those tricky out-of-bounds areas.

Understanding this rule will make your game smoother and more enjoyable. As you learn to confidently navigate the course, you’ll be building a strong foundation for your future golfing adventures!

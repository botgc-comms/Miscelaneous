# Quick Check

As you continue your golfing journey, it's important to revisit some key concepts before hitting the course. This quick check is designed to reinforce your understanding of the basic rules and ensure you’re prepared for your next round. 

### Key Points to Remember:

1. **Play the Ball as it Lies**: Always play the ball from where it rests, unless a rule allows you to move it.

2. **Out of Bounds (OB)**: If your ball lands out of bounds, you must take a penalty stroke and drop a new ball within the area designated by the course, usually near where it crossed the boundary line.

3. **Penalties and Drops**: Understand the types of penalties (like loss of stroke or distance) and the proper procedure for dropping the ball when a penalty occurs.

4. **Order of Play**: Generally, the player furthest from the hole plays first, but this can vary in casual play, so be sure to communicate with fellow golfers.

### Questions to Consider:

- What should you do if your ball is in a bunker or a hazard?
- How do you identify if you’ve landed in a 'Water Hazard'?

### Practical Exercise:

Before heading out, pair up with a friend or family member and quiz each other on these rules. Role-play different scenarios to practise decision-making in real-time situations on the course. This will help build confidence as you start playing! 

Keep these points in mind, and you will be well on your way to becoming a skilled junior golfer. Next up: we’ll explore etiquette—an essential part of the game!

# Order of Play

In golf, knowing the order of play is crucial for keeping the game flowing smoothly and ensuring fairness. Here’s how it works:

## Teeing Off

1. **Who Goes First?**  
   At the start of the game, the player with the lowest score on the previous hole tees off first. This is known as the "honour." If it’s the first hole, a random method can be used to decide who goes first, such as flipping a coin.

2. **Teeing Area:**  
   Make sure your ball is within the designated tee box area. A common mistake is to tee off from outside the marked zone, which can lead to penalties.

## Subsequent Shots

3. **Where the Ball Lies:**  
   After the tee shots, the player whose ball is farthest from the hole takes their shot first. This keeps the game moving and is easy to remember—just play your shot when it's your turn!

4. **Order on the Fairway and Greens:**  
   Continue to follow the "farther from the hole, first to play" rule. On the green, the player who is farthest from the hole also putts first. It’s a good idea to keep an eye on where everyone else’s ball is, so you can be ready when it’s your turn.

## Other Considerations

5. **Respect Others' Shots:**  
   Being ready to play not only helps the game flow but also shows respect for your fellow players. This means being attentive and minimising distractions while others are taking their shots.

6. **Alternate Shots:**  
   If you’re playing in teams, such as in a ‘four-ball’ format, players alternate shots. Always ensure that the order is clear to everyone involved to avoid confusion.

By mastering the order of play, you’ll help your group enjoy a more organised and fun round of golf. Keep practising and soon it will become second nature!

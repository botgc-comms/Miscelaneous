# Why the app matters

The Burton Junior Golf app has been designed to support both junior golfers and their parents throughout their golfing journey. It acts as a central hub for learning, communication, coaching, safeguarding, and tracking progress, helping young players understand what they need to do next as they develop their skills and move through the junior programme.

## 1. Track Your Progress

Golf can sometimes feel overwhelming when you're starting out. The app helps juniors understand where they are in their journey, what they have already achieved, and what they need to do next to reach their next milestone.

Whether you're working towards a course assessment, your first handicap, or the next junior category, the app provides a clear picture of your progress.

## 2. Learn the Rules of Golf

Understanding the rules is an important part of becoming a golfer. The app includes rules quizzes and learning resources that help juniors build their knowledge in an engaging and accessible way.

Learning the rules not only prepares juniors for competitions but also helps build confidence when playing on the course.

## 3. Access Learning Packs and Guidance

The app provides learning packs and supporting information for both juniors and parents. These resources are designed to help families understand the different stages of the junior pathway and provide practical guidance on how parents can support their child's development.

## 4. Celebrate Achievements

As juniors reach important milestones, the app recognises their achievements. Whether it is completing a course assessment, earning a new award, gaining a handicap, or reaching another significant goal, the app helps celebrate success and keep motivation high.

## 5. Earn Rewards and Vouchers

Many achievements are linked to rewards. Juniors can earn vouchers through the programme which can be redeemed for selected items and experiences through the golf club.

These rewards recognise effort, commitment, learning, and participation.

## 6. Access Coaching Opportunities

The app helps juniors discover coaching opportunities available through the club. Where coaching sessions are provided free of charge as part of the junior programme, details and eligibility can be managed through the app.

## 7. Stay Connected

Important information can be delivered directly through the app. Juniors and parents can receive messages about coaching sessions, competitions, events, achievements, and programme updates.

Where appropriate, juniors can also contact their assigned junior mentor through the app for guidance and support.

## 8. Support Safeguarding

The safety and wellbeing of our juniors is our highest priority. The app allows parents to provide safeguarding information that helps the club understand any important information needed to support their child safely and appropriately.

Keeping this information up to date helps us provide the best possible environment for every junior golfer.

## 9. Parents Stay Involved

Everything that a junior can see within the app is also visible to their parent or guardian. Parents can monitor progress, view achievements, access learning materials, receive messages, and stay informed about their child's development.

This shared visibility helps families support their child throughout their golfing journey.

# Making the Most of the Voucher Scheme

The voucher scheme is designed to reward participation, learning, and achievement. By engaging with the app, completing milestones, and taking part in the junior programme, juniors can earn vouchers that can be redeemed through the club.

Combined with coaching opportunities, learning resources, and progress tracking, the voucher scheme helps make the golfing journey both rewarding and enjoyable.

The more you engage with the programme, the more opportunities you'll have to learn, develop, and enjoy everything that junior golf has to offer.

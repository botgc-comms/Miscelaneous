# Learning

The **Learning** section helps juniors build their knowledge of the Rules of Golf in a fun and engaging way.

Juniors can access rules quizzes at any time and work through them at their own pace. If a quiz is not completed in one sitting, progress is saved so it can be continued later.

Each quiz requires a score of at least **70%** to pass. The questions presented are tailored to the junior's current level of experience, helping them learn the rules that are most relevant to their stage of development.

Every junior is entitled to a free Rules of Golf booklet using one of their vouchers. We encourage juniors to use this booklet when completing quizzes, as learning how to find and interpret the rules is just as important as knowing the answers themselves.

By regularly using the Learning section, juniors will develop the confidence to understand and apply the Rules of Golf when they are out on the course.

# During the Round

Now that you're prepared for your first competition, it’s time to focus on what happens during the round. This is where you’ll put your skills to the test and truly enjoy the game of golf!

### Stay Calm and Focused

It’s natural to feel a bit nervous before and during your round. Here are some tips to help you stay calm:

- **Breathe**: Take a few deep breaths to centre yourself.
- **Stay Positive**: Keep a positive mindset. Remember, each shot is a new opportunity!

### Keeping Track of Your Score

Understanding how to keep score is essential. Here’s how:

1. **Count Each Stroke**: Every time you swing the club, that counts as one stroke.
2. **Use a Scorecard**: Your coach or an older player can help you fill in your scorecard correctly.
3. **Mark Your Ball**: If your ball goes into a hazard (like a water hazard or bunker), remember to take note of any penalties. 

### Etiquette on the Course

Golf is as much about respect for others as it is about your play. Here are some important etiquette tips:

- **Be Quiet**: When someone is preparing to hit, remain quiet and still.
- **Don’t Distract Others**: Avoid moving or talking to your friends while they are taking their shots.
- **Respect the Course**: Replace divots, rake bunkers, and repair pitch marks on the greens. Taking care of the course is part of being a good golfer!

### Dealing with Mistakes

Remember, everyone makes mistakes—including professional golfers! Here’s how to handle it:

- **Stay Composed**: If a shot doesn’t go as planned, take a moment to regroup.
- **Learn from It**: Instead of dwelling on mistakes, think about what you can learn for your next shot.
- **Enjoy the Experience**: Focus on having fun. Golf is a journey, and each round is an adventure!

### Using Your Caddie

If you’re playing with a caddie, they can be a great resource. Here’s how to make the most of the experience:

- **Ask Questions**: Don’t hesitate to ask your caddie for advice on clubs and shot strategy.
- **Listen**: Take in their recommendations, but trust your own instincts as well.

### The Spirit of Competition

Competitions are about more than just winning. It’s about learning, making friends, and enjoying the game. Embrace the spirit of sportsmanship by congratulating others on their good shots and showing kindness.

As you navigate your round, focus on enjoying the game and taking pride in your performance. Your journey in golf is just beginning, and every round will bring new experiences and lessons!

# Meet the Team

As you embark on your golfing adventure, it's vital to know the friendly faces who will support you along the way. Understanding who is involved in your learning will help you feel more comfortable and connected to your new sport.

### Coaches

Our dedicated coaches are here to ensure that you not only learn the fundamentals of golf but also enjoy every moment on the course. Each coach is passionate about helping junior golfers develop their skills and confidence. Here’s a brief introduction to each:

- **Coach Jamie**: With over a decade of coaching experience, Jamie has a knack for making lessons fun and engaging. They focus on building a solid foundation in technique while encouraging a love for the game.

- **Coach Alex**: Known for their enthusiasm, Alex makes every session exciting. They specialise in helping young golfers understand the rules and etiquette of the game, ensuring a well-rounded experience.

### Support Staff

Beyond the coaches, our support staff plays an essential role in your learning journey. They help manage the logistics of sessions, ensuring everything runs smoothly.

- **Tom, the Events Coordinator**: Tom organises friendly tournaments and events, offering valuable opportunities for practice and competition. Look out for announcements about upcoming events – they’re a great way to showcase what you’ve learned!

- **Sarah, our Parent Liaison**: Sarah is here to assist parents with any queries or concerns. She keeps everyone informed about schedules and progress, ensuring a community atmosphere.

### Learning Environment

Our team is committed to creating a supportive and encouraging environment. We celebrate progress, big or small, and strive to make every golfer feel valued. Everyone is here for the same purpose: to enjoy the game of golf and grow together.

As you continue through your junior pathway, remember that you’re not just learning a sport; you’re becoming part of a community. Embrace the journey and always feel free to reach out to any of us for guidance or assistance. Let’s tee off on this exciting adventure together!

# Course Cadet Stage

The Course Cadet stage is an exciting phase where young golfers begin to play on a real golf course. This level encourages both skill development and an understanding of course etiquette, helping to prepare juniors for enjoyable rounds with friends and family. 

## Understanding the Course

### Key Areas to Learn:
1. **Course Layout**
   - Familiarise yourself with the layout of the course: tees, fairways, greens, and hazards.
   - Learn to read the scorecard to understand par values, distances, and hole layouts.

2. **Teeing Off**
   - Understand the importance of starting each hole from the tee box. 
   - Aim for a clean strike off the tee, choosing the correct club based on distance and difficulty.

3. **Navigating Hazards**
   - Identify areas like bunkers and water hazards. 
   - Learn basic rules for playing out of these areas, including taking a penalty stroke if necessary.

4. **Putting on Greens**
   - Develop a feel for distance control and aim when on the putting green.
   - Practice reading the greens to understand slope and speed.

### Basic Rules to Remember:
- Always be aware of playing order: the player farthest from the hole plays first.
- Repair any damage made to the course, such as divots or ball marks.
- Keep pace with the group in front of you to ensure a smooth flow during play.

## Playing in a Group

### Etiquette Essentials:
- Be respectful of other players: keep noise to a minimum and avoid distractions.
- Stay ready to play when it’s your turn.
- Be encouraging and supportive of your fellow golfers, celebrating successes and helping with challenges.

## Fun Activities:
- Organise a friendly round with fellow Cadets or family members.
- Keep a scorecard to track progress – celebrate personal victories!
- Create mini-challenges, such as closest to the pin or longest drive to make the game more exciting.

As you navigate this stage, the focus is on both fun and learning. With practice and time, you'll build confidence and become more comfortable on the course. Let's keep growing those golfing skills!

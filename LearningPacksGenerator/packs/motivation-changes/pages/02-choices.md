# Giving Choice

Involving young golfers in decisions about their game can significantly enhance their motivation and enjoyment. When juniors feel they have a say in their golfing journey, they're more likely to embrace challenges and strive for improvement. Here are some ways to effectively give choices:

### 1. **Set Personal Goals**
Encourage your young golfer to set their own targets. These can be simple, such as aiming for a certain number of putts per round or trying to improve their swing technique. Allowing them to choose goals ensures they are meaningful and engaging.

### 2. **Select Practice Drills**
Rather than dictating practice routines, present a range of drills and let them choose which ones to focus on. This not only builds ownership but also helps them discover which aspects of their game they are most passionate about developing.

### 3. **Course Management Decisions**
When out on the course, encourage your junior golfer to think critically about each shot. Ask them questions like, “What club do you think is best for this hole?” or “Where would you like to aim?” This promotes strategic thinking and fosters independence.

### 4. **Playing Partners**
Involve them in deciding who they would like to play with. Whether it's friends, family members, or other young golfers, their choice of playing partners can greatly influence their enjoyment and comfort on the course.

### 5. **Fun Variations**
Introduce creative formats for practice or play. Allowing juniors to choose from options such as team games, challenges, or skills contests adds an element of fun and can reignite enthusiasm for the sport.

### Conclusion
By providing choices, you not only cultivate a sense of autonomy in young golfers but also nurture their love for the game. This empowerment helps them to own their journey, making the process of learning and growing in golf even more rewarding. Encourage them to make decisions, and you will be amazed at the positive outcomes that follow!

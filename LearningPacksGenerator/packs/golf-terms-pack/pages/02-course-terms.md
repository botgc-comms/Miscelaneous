# Course Words

Congratulations on moving forward in your golfing journey! Understanding the language of the course is essential, as it will enhance your experience and communication while playing. Let’s explore some common words and terms you might encounter on the golf course.

## 1. Fairway
The area of short grass between the tee box and the green. This is where you ideally want your ball to land after your tee shot, as it provides the best lie for your next shot.

## 2. Rough
The longer grass that borders the fairway. Hitting your ball into the rough can make it harder to play, so it’s best to aim for the fairway.

## 3. Green
The area of closely trimmed grass surrounding the hole, where players putt to complete the hole. This is where you’ll end up trying to make your final stroke!

## 4. Tee Box
The starting area for each hole. Each hole has a designated tee box from which players begin their play.

## 5. Bunker
A sand trap located on the course, typically near the greens. If your ball lands in a bunker, you'll need to use a special technique to get it back onto the course.

## 6. Hole
The target for your golf ball. Each hole has a specific distance and layout, culminating at the flagstick planted in the green.

## 7. Par
The number of strokes an expert golfer is expected to take to complete a hole. Each hole has a specific par, ranging from 3 to 5 strokes.

## 8. Fairway Wood
A type of club designed for long-distance shots from the fairway. These clubs are ideal for getting distance while maintaining accuracy.

## 9. Flagstick
A pole with a flag that marks the location of the hole on the green. The flagstick is removed when the ball is being putted.

## 10. Course
The entire layout of 18 holes (or fewer) designed for playing golf. Each course is unique in its design and challenges.

### Practice Makes Perfect
Try to familiarise yourself with these terms as you walk the course. Knowing the language will make your golf experience much smoother and more enjoyable!

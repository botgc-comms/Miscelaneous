# Quick check

Now that you’re familiar with the dress code and etiquette, it’s time to see how much you’ve absorbed! Use these quick questions to reinforce what you’ve learned. Each question is a chance to solidify your knowledge and ensure you’re ready for the golf course.

### True or False?

1. **A golf shirt with a collar is optional when playing.**  
   *True or False?*

2. **It’s acceptable to talk loudly when your playing partner is about to take a shot.**  
   *True or False?*

3. **Wearing trainers is completely fine on the golf course.**  
   *True or False?*

4. **Respecting the course and its maintenance staff is part of good etiquette.**  
   *True or False?*

### A Quick Scenario

Imagine you’re about to play a round of golf with friends. As you arrive at the first tee, you notice one of your friends is wearing jeans and a t-shirt. How do you think this affects your group’s experience and the atmosphere on the course? Write down your thoughts!

### Fill in the Blanks

Complete these sentences to further test your understanding:

1. It’s important to arrive at the course _________ before your tee time.
2. If you’re unsure about the dress code, it’s always best to _________.
3. When on the green, remember to remain _________ and avoid moving around too much.

### Reflection

Take a moment to reflect on your own experiences. Think about a time when you noticed someone following or breaking etiquette on the course. What did you learn from that situation? Write a few sentences to describe it.

---

As you consider these questions and scenarios, remember that understanding and practicing good etiquette makes you a better golfer and a better teammate. Prepare yourself for the next chapter in your golf learning journey!

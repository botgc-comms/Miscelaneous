# On the Green

Congratulations on making it to the putting surface! This is where the game becomes truly exciting, as every stroke counts and patience pays off. Here are some essential tips to keep in mind while on the green:

### Silence is Golden
When you're on the green, it's vital to respect fellow players. Maintain a quiet atmosphere, allowing everyone to concentrate. Avoid talking while someone is preparing to putt, and refrain from making noise that could distract them.

### Stay Behind the Putter
Always position yourself behind the player who is about to take their shot. This ensures you won't block their line of sight to the hole and allows you to observe their technique while showing proper etiquette.

### Marking Your Ball
If your ball is in the way of another player’s putt, it’s courteous to mark it. Use a coin or a specific marker to note your ball's position, lift it carefully, and replace it exactly where it was after the other player has taken their shot.

### Assessing the Green
Take a moment to read the green before you putt. Look for slopes, grain, or other features that might affect your shot. Remember, it’s important to respect the player's time, so be prepared to take your turn promptly.

### Repairing the Green
If your ball leaves a mark on the green or if there are any divots, be sure to repair them using a pitchmark repair tool. This shows respect for the course and helps keep it in great condition for everyone.

### Take Your Place
When it’s your turn to putt, step up confidently. Focus on your shot, align your putter with the ball, and remember the fundamentals you've learned – stance, grip, and follow-through can make all the difference!

### Celebrate Responsibly
If your putt sinks, a simple fist pump or smile is great. Avoid overly exuberant celebrations that might distract or disturb other players on the green. Keep the spirit of the game in mind!

By mastering these points of etiquette on the green, you’ll not only enjoy your game more but also contribute to a positive atmosphere for all players around you. Keep practicing, and soon you’ll be sinking those putts with confidence!

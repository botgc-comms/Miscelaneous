# Quick Check

Safety is paramount when you're out on the golf course. To ensure a fun and secure experience, let’s quickly review some essential safety tips.

### 1. **Check Your Equipment**
   - Ensure your clubs are in good condition. Look for cracks or chips, especially on the heads.
   - Always have a good grip on your clubs to prevent them from slipping from your hands, especially on a wet day.

### 2. **Be Aware of Your Surroundings**
   - Before making a swing, take a moment to look around. Check if anyone is in the line of fire, both ahead and behind you.
   - Watch out for other players and their shots. It’s good practice to always be mindful of where others are on the course.

### 3. **Keep a Safe Distance**
   - Stand behind the player taking their shot. The space between you and them should be at least two club lengths to stay safe.
   - When putting or in close proximity to other players, continue to respect personal space and keep distractions minimal.

### 4. **Stay Alert**
   - Always pay attention while walking on the course. Look out for uneven ground, especially in areas where you might trip.
   - If you hear thunder or see lightning, seek shelter immediately. Don’t wait to finish your game; your safety is more important.

### 5. **Respect the Course**
   - Avoid playing in areas that are marked as ‘out of bounds’ or displayed with caution signs. These are there for your protection.
   - Take care when near water hazards and bunkers. Slippery surfaces can lead to accidents.

### Final Thought
Staying safe is a vital part of enjoying golf. Remember these checks before you step onto the course, and you’ll create a positive environment for yourself and your fellow golfers. After all, playing safely means you can concentrate on improving those swings and enjoying the game!

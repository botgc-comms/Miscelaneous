# Before you start

Now that you’re excited to hit the course, let’s make sure you understand how to stay safe while enjoying the game of golf. Being aware of your surroundings and following a few simple guidelines will help ensure a fun and secure experience for everyone.

### 1. Understand Your Environment
- **Be Observant:** Always be aware of other golfers around you. Keep an eye on where they are hitting from, and make sure you wait your turn before taking your shot.
- **Watch for Obstacles:** Be mindful of trees, water hazards, and bunkers. These can pose risks, so stay alert and plan your shots safely.

### 2. Use the Right Gear
- **Wear Proper Attire:** Comfortable clothing and golf shoes will help you move freely. Don’t forget a cap and sunscreen to protect yourself from the sun!
- **Safety Glasses**: Consider wearing glasses to protect your eyes from flying debris or errant golf balls.

### 3. Stay in Control
- **Swing Safely:** When preparing to hit, always check that no one is in your line of fire. It’s important to shout “Fore!” if your ball goes off course and is heading towards someone.
- **Move with Care:** When walking the course, be mindful of slippery spots and uneven ground, particularly after rain.

### 4. Follow Course Etiquette
- **Respect Others' Space:** Maintain a respectful distance from other players. Stand quietly while they are about to hit and avoid making distracting noises.
- **Pick Up After Yourself:** Dispose of any rubbish and return any borrowed equipment to its rightful place. This keeps the course clean and shows respect for the game.

### 5. Seek Help If Needed
- **Ask an Adult for Guidance:** If you're unsure about anything on the course, don’t hesitate to ask a parent, coach, or experienced player for assistance. Everyone was a beginner once!

By being mindful and following these safety tips, you can enjoy your golfing experience while ensuring a safe environment for everyone on the course. Next, let’s dive into some fundamental techniques that will help you play better golf!

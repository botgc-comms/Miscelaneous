# Shouting ‘Fore!’

As you continue your golfing journey, it's essential to understand the importance of safety on the course. One of the key safety practices is knowing when and how to shout ‘Fore!’. This simple word can help prevent accidents and ensure everyone enjoys their game.

## What does ‘Fore!’ mean?

When you shout ‘Fore!’, you are warning other players or spectators that a golf ball may be heading their way. This helps them take cover or be alert, preventing injuries. It’s a signal that should never be ignored on the course.

## When should you shout?

You should shout ‘Fore!’ in the following situations:

- **If your shot goes in an unexpected direction**, particularly towards other people.
- **If someone else’s ball is heading towards you**, and you want to warn your teammates or other players nearby.

## How to shout it effectively

- **Be loud and clear**: Make sure your voice carries, just like a good golf swing!
- **Aim for a direct line**: Shout in the direction of where you think the ball might land.
- **Always be aware**: Keep an eye on your surroundings and look out for others while playing.

## Practising Safety Together

As a junior golfer, remember that safety is everyone’s responsibility. If you see someone else’s ball heading towards a group, don’t hesitate to shout ‘Fore!’. Encourage your friends to do the same. You’re not only protecting yourself but also helping to create a safe environment for everyone on the course.

## Activity: Role Play

With a family member or a friend, practise shouting ‘Fore!’ in a safe and fun way. Take turns pretending to hit the ball in different directions, and see how loud and clear you can shout. It’s a great way to get comfortable with the term!

Remember, a good golfer is always aware of their surroundings and prioritises safety on the course. This small act can make a big difference in enjoying the game together!

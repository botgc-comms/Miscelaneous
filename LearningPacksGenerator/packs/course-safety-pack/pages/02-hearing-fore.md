# If Someone Shouts ‘Fore!’

Understanding course etiquette is essential for enjoying golf while staying safe. One important term to remember is ‘Fore!’ This shout is a friendly warning to alert others when a ball is heading in their direction.

### What to Do When You Hear ‘Fore!’

1. **Stay Alert**: Always be aware of your surroundings. If you’re on the course, keep an eye on other players, especially those ahead of you.
  
2. **Move Out of the Way**: As soon as you hear someone shout ‘Fore!’, try to quickly move to a safer spot, either behind a tree or off to the side of the fairway.

3. **Protect Yourself**: If you can’t get out of the way, protect your head and face. Use your arms to shield yourself and bend down if necessary.

4. **Stay Calm**: Accidents happen, and sometimes people shout ‘Fore!’ even if the ball won’t hit anyone. Take a deep breath, and don’t panic.

### Why ‘Fore!’ Is Important

The shout serves as a crucial safety measure, helping to prevent injuries on the golf course. It encourages communication among players and fosters a sense of community and respect. Just remember, when you hear it, it's time to act!

### Practice Makes Perfect

Have a chat with your coach or family about the importance of shouting ‘Fore!’ and how to respond. The more you learn and practice these safety measures, the more comfortable you’ll feel playing golf.

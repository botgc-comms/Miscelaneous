# Temporary Rules

As the golf season changes, so do certain aspects of the game. These temporary rules will help you navigate the unique challenges presented by winter conditions, ensuring that everyone can enjoy their time on the course safely and fairly. Here’s what you need to know:

## 1. Lift, Clean and Place

During winter, the ground may become muddy or frosty, affecting your ball's lie. If your ball is in a condition that isn't ideal for play:

- **Lift** your ball carefully.
- **Clean** it off to remove any mud or debris.
- **Place** it back on the ground within one club length of where it originally lay, but not nearer to the hole.

## 2. Keeping the Course in Good Condition

To help protect the course during winter weather:

- Avoid walking on frosty grass.
- Don’t play from areas that are especially damaged or muddy.
- Stay on paths where available to minimise wear and tear.

## 3. Preferred Lies

In some winter conditions, you might have the option to use 'preferred lies.' This allows you to improve your position:

- If you find your ball in an area with snow or wet ground, you can move it to a dry patch within a specified distance, usually no more than six inches.

## 4. Scorecards and Winter Competitions

If you’re playing in club competitions, be aware that special rules may apply. Sometimes, scores for these events might be adjusted to account for weather conditions or course maintenance. Always check with the organisers for any specific local rules.

## 5. Practice and Play Timing

As the days grow shorter, you’ll need to adjust your practice and play times. Consider:

- Playing earlier in the day when temperatures are milder.
- Ensuring you have enough daylight to finish your round.

Understanding and adapting to these temporary rules will help you maintain your game and keep enjoying golf through the winter months. Let’s look forward to improving your skills in these challenging conditions!

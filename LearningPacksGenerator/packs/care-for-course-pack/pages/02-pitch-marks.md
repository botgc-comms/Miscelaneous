# Pitch Marks on Greens

As new golfers, it's essential to understand not only how to play the game but also how to look after the course we love. One common issue you’ll encounter on the putting greens is pitch marks. These are indentations caused by the impact of a golf ball when it lands on the green.

### Why Are Pitch Marks Important?

Pitch marks can disrupt the smooth surface of the green, making it difficult for putts to roll true. By learning how to repair them properly, you’re doing your part to maintain the course for everyone to enjoy.

### How to Repair a Pitch Mark

1. **Identify the Mark:** Look for small dents on the green. They may appear as tiny craters with grass that has been pushed upwards.

2. **Use a Repair Tool:** Most golf courses provide repair tools, but you can also use a tee if needed. Hold the tool at a 45-degree angle to the edge of the mark.

3. **Lift the Turf:** Gently lift the edge of the pitch mark towards the centre. Be sure not to push down too hard, as this can damage the grass further.

4. **Press Down:** Once you've lifted the edges, gently press down on the centre to level it with the surrounding surface. This helps the grass to heal and grow back properly.

5. **Finish Off:** Lastly, give the area a light tap with your putter or foot to ensure everything is flat.

### Quick Tips:

- Always fix your pitch mark and any others you see—helping out helps everyone!
- When you finish repairing, take a look around. The greens will be better for it, and it shows respect for the game and other players.

### Fun Fact:

Did you know that a well-maintained green can be the difference in putting? A smooth surface ensures that putts roll true towards the hole. 

Next time you’re out playing, remember how important it is to care for the greens, and you'll be a valuable part of the golfing community!

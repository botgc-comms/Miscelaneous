# Tools and Habits

As you continue your journey in golf, it’s crucial to develop good habits and understand the tools you'll need to care for the golf course. These practices not only enhance your play but also ensure that everyone can enjoy the course.

## Essential Tools

1. **Pitch Mark Repair Tool**: This handy tool is designed to fix the small dents you may make on the green with your ball. Repairing pitch marks promptly helps keep the surface smooth and playable for others.

2. **Divot Repair Tool**: Similar to the pitch mark repair tool, this allows you to fix any divots taken from the fairway. Make sure to replace the turf and tamp it down gently with your foot.

3. **Bunker Rake**: After playing from a bunker, it’s important to leave it in good condition. Use the rake to smooth the sand, ensuring others have a fair lie next time they enter.

4. **Tees**: These help you lift the ball off the ground for your first stroke. Make sure to only use them on the tee box and avoid leaving them scattered around the course.

## Good Habits

- **Always repair the course**: After each shot, take a moment to fix any marks you’ve made. This shows respect for the course and helps your fellow golfers.
  
- **Be mindful of where you walk**: Try to avoid walking on greens around the hole and use the paths provided whenever possible.

- **Keep the course tidy**: If you see litter, including food wrappers or empty drinks, pick it up and dispose of it properly. Keeping the environment clean benefits everyone.

- **Practice courtesy**: Always be aware of your surroundings and how your actions may impact other players. For example, wait until the group ahead has moved on before taking your shot.

As you become familiar with these tools and habits, you'll not only improve your golf game but also contribute positively to the course. Your efforts will help ensure that future golfers can enjoy the same beautiful playing conditions you do! Happy golfing!

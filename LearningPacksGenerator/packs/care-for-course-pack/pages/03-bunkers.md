# Raking Bunkers

As you explore the golf course, you’ll come across bunkers—those sandy traps designed to test your skills. Understanding how to maintain these areas not only helps the next player but also keeps the course looking its best. Let’s dive into how to rake a bunker properly!

### Why Rake?

Raking bunkers is essential for keeping the sand smooth and playable. If everyone does their part, it ensures a fair playing experience for everyone. Plus, it helps keep the course tidy!

### How to Rake a Bunker

1. **Enter the Bunker Carefully**: Stepping into a bunker can cause the sand to shift dramatically. Approach from the low side to minimise disturbance.

2. **Play Your Shot**: After hitting your ball, it’s time to tidy up. Take a moment to assess the sand where your shot landed.

3. **Use the Rake Properly**: 
   - **Rake from the Outside In**: Start at the edge of the bunker and move towards the spot where the ball was. This keeps the disturbed area contained and allows for easier smoothing.
   - **Smooth Out the Sand**: Use the rake to spread the sand evenly, filling in any depressions.

4. **Exit with Care**: As you leave the bunker, avoid standing on the edges to prevent creating footprints. Exit from the low side, similar to how you entered.

5. **Return the Rake**: Place the rake back in the bunker, either parallel to the bunker's edge or outside (depending on the course rules). Always ensure it isn’t in the path of another player’s shot.

### Additional Tips

- **Be Mindful of Others**: Always check if anyone is preparing to play before entering a bunker. Safety first!
- **Practice Makes Perfect**: Raking might seem simple, but doing it consistently will make a noticeable difference in the course’s condition.

By raking bunkers after your turn, you enhance your experience and contribute to the enjoyment of everyone else. A little care goes a long way in fostering respect for the game and the course.

# Quick Check

As you continue your journey in golf, it’s vital to ensure that the course remains in top condition. Let’s review some simple ways that you can personally contribute to protecting and respecting the course during your play. 

### Essential Course Care Checklist

1. **Divots**: 
   - Have you replaced any divots you’ve taken from the fairway? Use the grass you removed to fill the hole, ensuring the area can recover quickly.
  
2. **Pitch Marks**: 
   - After you take a shot on the green, have you repaired any pitch marks? Gently lift the edges of the mark with a tee or pitchmark repair tool for a smoother putting surface.

3. **Bunkers**: 
   - Once you’ve played from a bunker, have you raked it neatly? Ensure the sand is evenly distributed and free from footprints.

4. **General Litter**: 
   - Have you picked up any litter you came across during your round? Keeping the course tidy shows respect and helps maintain its beauty.

5. **Grooming Equipment**: 
   - Do you take care of your clubs and equipment? Make sure they're clean after every round to avoid damage.

### Why This Matters

Maintaining the course isn’t just about following rules; it’s about being a good golfer and showing respect for those who play after you. A well-cared-for course enhances everyone’s experience and helps preserve our green spaces for future generations.

### Reflection

Take a moment to think about your round today. Did you do everything you could to care for the course? Keep these points in mind during your next game, and encourage your fellow juniors to do the same. Happy golfing!

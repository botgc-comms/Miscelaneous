# Stableford Scoring

Stableford is a fun and rewarding way to score while playing golf. Unlike traditional stroke play, where every stroke counts towards your total, Stableford allows you to earn points based on your performance on each hole. This system encourages players to take risks and enjoy the game without the pressure of a high total score.

## How Does It Work?

In a Stableford competition, you earn points based on the number of strokes it takes you to finish each hole compared to a pre-determined score known as the "par". Here’s a simple breakdown:

- **Par**: The number of strokes a skilled golfer is expected to take to complete a hole.
- **Points System**:
  - **0 points**: If you take more strokes than your par plus two strokes (e.g., a 7 on a par 5).
  - **1 point**: If you equal par (e.g., 5 on a par 5).
  - **2 points**: If you take one stroke less than par (e.g., 4 on a par 5).
  - **3 points**: If you take two strokes less than par (e.g., 3 on a par 5).
  - **4 points**: If you take three strokes less than par (e.g., 2 on a par 5).
  
## Why Use Stableford?

Stableford scoring makes golf enjoyable and less stressful, especially for beginners. Here are a few reasons why it’s a great choice for junior golfers:

- **Encourages Positive Play**: Players are rewarded for good play, motivating them to improve.
- **Less Pressure**: Missing a few shots won’t ruin your round, allowing for a more relaxed experience.
- **Focus on Skill Development**: It fosters growth by promoting risk-taking for better scores.

## Tips for Success

1. **Understand Your Course's Par**: Familiarise yourself with the par for each hole. This helps in assessing your performance.
2. **Course Management**: Think about your shots; sometimes, playing it safe is the best strategy.
3. **Stay Positive**: Celebrate your good holes and remember that not every shot will be perfect.

By understanding Stableford scoring, you can elevate your enjoyment and engagement with the game. Encourage your child to embrace this format and let them see the joy in improving their skills with every round played. Keep practicing, and soon enough, they might just be racking up those points!

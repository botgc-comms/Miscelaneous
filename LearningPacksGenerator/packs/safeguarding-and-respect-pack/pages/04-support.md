# Getting Support

In your journey as a junior golfer, feeling supported is paramount. Whether you're tackling your first swing or navigating the course, knowing you can reach out for help is essential.

## Who Can Help?

1. **Coaches**  
   Your coach is your first point of contact. They’re not just there to teach you golf skills, but also to ensure you feel safe and empowered. If you have any concerns or need guidance, don’t hesitate to speak to them.

2. **Parents and Guardians**  
   Always keep your parents or guardians in the loop. They can provide additional support and understanding of your feelings and experiences in the game.

3. **Friends**  
   Fellow junior golfers can be a great support network. Sharing your thoughts and challenges can help you feel more connected and understood.

## How to Ask for Help

- **Be Honest**:  It’s perfectly okay to share what’s on your mind. If something bothers you, speaking up can lead to solutions.
  
- **Choose the Right Moment**: Find a calm and appropriate time to approach someone for support, whether it's during practice or after a game.

- **Use Open Questions**: When asking for help, open questions like “Can you help me with this?” invite more detailed responses and support.

## What to Talk About

- **Any Feelings of Worry or Discomfort**: Don’t brush aside your feelings. If something doesn’t feel right, it’s important to share it.
  
- **Skill Struggles**: Talking about areas where you need improvement can often lead to helpful advice and support.

- **Friendship Challenges**: Golf is a social sport. If you’re struggling with friendships on the course, reach out to someone who can help.

## Remember

Getting support isn’t a sign of weakness; it’s a brave step towards growth and confidence. With the right support network, you can enjoy your golf journey fully and safely. Always remember, you're not alone—there are many people ready to help you succeed on and off the course!

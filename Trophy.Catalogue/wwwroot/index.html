Frequently asked questions

Before you open the trophy cabinet.

Will it read every engraving perfectly?

No. Reflections, damage and unusual lettering can remain ambiguous. The service combines several views, shows confidence and requires human review rather than pretending uncertainty does not exist.

Can we add a trophy that is not already listed?

Yes. Every new club begins with no trophies. Select “Add trophy”, enter its details, take or upload photographs, and the app creates both its archive record and catalogue illustration.

Can we add missing winners ourselves?

Yes. Set an expected year range to expose gaps, then add or correct any winner manually using information from minutes, honours boards or other club records.

Does it work outside golf?

Yes. The same workflow suits rugby, cricket, tennis, bowls and football clubs, schools, military associations, charities and museums.

What happens to member dates of birth?

The importer derives only a birth year, then discards the day and month. This helps reject impossible historical matches without retaining unnecessary personal data.

Can we export the finished archive?

Yes. Trophy details, winners, review status and optional member matches can be downloaded as CSV for use in another system.

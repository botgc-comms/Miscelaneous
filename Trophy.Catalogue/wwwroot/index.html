Questions clubs ask first

Clear answers before you open the cabinet.

Will every engraving be read perfectly?

No. Reflections, damage and handwriting-style engraving can remain ambiguous. The service uses several views, confidence scores and a required human review rather than pretending uncertainty does not exist.

Can we add winners from other records?

Yes. Add a winner manually, choose a known missing year, or correct an AI suggestion. Confirmed human records are protected from later automated replacement.

What happens to member dates of birth?

The import uses the date only to derive a birth year, then discards the day and month. Birth year helps reject impossible matches without keeping more personal data than the workflow needs.

Does it work outside golf?

Yes. Cups, shields, medals and plaques from rugby, cricket, tennis, bowls, football, schools, military associations, charities and museums use the same evidence-and-review workflow.

Do we own the results?

Your organisation can export the reconstructed winners list as CSV at any time. A production account should also include deletion and retention controls for uploaded evidence and member data.

Can this publish directly to our club website?

Not yet. Website and Intelligent Golf publishing are planned as separately priced modules after the core archive service is established.
